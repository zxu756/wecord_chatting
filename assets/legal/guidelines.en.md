# Community Guidelines

WeCord is a place to talk with friends, groups and circles. These guidelines say what is not welcome, so that everyone can feel at home. They apply to everything you send, post, upload or say on WeCord: messages, photos, voice and video, circle posts, Moments, profiles and names.

## Be kind

- **No harassment or bullying.** Do not insult, threaten, stalk or repeatedly target a person, and do not encourage others to.
- **No hate.** Do not attack people for who they are: race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, sex, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or illness.
- **No violence.** Do not threaten violence, praise it, or share content made to shock with real suffering.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not share other people's private information (addresses, phone numbers, ID documents, private photos or conversations) without their consent. Do not record or share a call without telling the others.

## Keep it safe

- **Nothing that sexualises minors.** This is never allowed, and we report it to the authorities where the law requires it.
- **No sexual content you were not asked to send.** Adult content is not allowed in public circles or in anything sent to someone who did not ask for it.
- **No illegal activity.** Do not use WeCord to sell or promote illegal goods or services, or to plan or organise harm.
- **Self-harm.** If you are thinking about harming yourself, please reach out to someone you trust or a local helpline. Content that encourages self-harm is not allowed.

## Be honest

- **No spam or scams.** Do not send unwanted bulk messages, chain invitations, phishing links, fake giveaways or pyramid schemes.
- **No impersonation.** Do not pretend to be another person, a moderator or WeCord staff.
- **No malicious files or links.** Do not send malware or links meant to steal accounts or data.
- **Bots and automation** must follow the same rules, must say what they are, and must not be used to get around limits.

## In circles

Each circle may add its own rules on top of these guidelines. Circle owners and moderators can delete messages, time members out, remove or ban them, and use automatic moderation. Circles that break these guidelines can be hidden from Discover or closed.

## Reporting

You can report:

- **a message** (open the message menu and choose Report). Reports from a circle go to that circle's moderators; reports from direct chats and groups go to the WeCord team. The reported person is not told who reported them;
- **a person** (from their profile);
- **a circle or a circle post.**

When you report a message, WeCord keeps a copy of it, and the messages around it if you agree, so that it can still be reviewed after it is deleted. Only the people who handle the report can read the copy, and it is erased 90 days after the report has been handled. Please report in good faith: repeated false reports may lead to action against your account. You can also block anyone at any time.

## What can happen

Depending on how serious the case is and whether it is repeated, we or a circle's moderators may remove content, warn you, time you out, remove you from a circle, suspend your account for a time or for good, or take a circle down. We may report illegal content to the authorities. If you think a decision was wrong, contact us at the address given in the Terms of Service.

## Changes

We may update these guidelines. When the changes matter, we will tell you in the app.
